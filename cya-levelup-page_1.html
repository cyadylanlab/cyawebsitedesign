Level Up Clinics

Optional supplemental training led by CYA staff coaches, indoors at the CYA Fieldhouse.

Supplemental Training Open Enrollment

Level Up is our new supplemental training program: additional coached sessions led by CYA staff, built to complement a recreational division or to join on its own. Enroll alongside your player’s division for extra time on the ball, or register as a standalone program with no rec season required. Every session is indoors at the CYA Fieldhouse, so a Thursday night never gets rained out.

The Programs

U4–U6 · Bantams

Bantam: Level Up

An extra night on the ball for our youngest players. Small groups, staff coaches, and sessions built around games rather than lines and drills, so players fall for the sport while they are busy playing it. We keep it fun and we keep the bar high. Every player is asked to try something just past what they can already do, which is exactly how a five-year-old learns the fundamentals and learns they are capable at the same time. Perfect alongside a Bantams season, and a great first step for a player who just wants more time playing.

Thursdays: Boys 5:00–6:00pm · Girls 6:00–7:00pm

U7–U8 · Academy

Valor Rising Lite

Virginia Valor FC’s open-enrollment development program for U7 and U8 players, with no tryout. Players train once a week with professional Valor staff on top of their CYA rec schedule. It runs as three separate seasons (fall, winter, and spring) that you can sign up for individually, and it is the entry point for families who may want to try out for Valor travel soccer at U9.

Fall season: once a week with professional Valor staff, in addition to your rec team schedule. Winter and spring are offered as separate seasons.

U9–U12 · Juniors

Junior: Level Up

For players who want to get noticeably better. Staff coaches drill the fundamentals that actually decide games: a first touch that buys you time, turning out of pressure, finishing, and quick decisions in tight spaces. Expect to be challenged. Players are pushed to compete, to get something wrong, and to go again, and the confidence that comes out of a night like that is earned rather than handed out. High tempo, small groups, and a standard every player is held to. Pairs with a Juniors season or stands on its own.

Thursdays: 7:00–8:00pm
